For that check, you get thirteen point five kilowatt-hours of usable storage and eleven point five kilowatts of continuous power output.</p><p>Crack open the casing, though, and you find something rivals simply do not give you: a fully integrated solar inverter. That is for a pack roughly one third the size of the Powerwall.</p><p>The engineering logic is real, though.
